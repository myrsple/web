Nanoclaw sales team

For my freelance practice I built an agentic CRM that generates, qualifies and cold-mails leads at scale.

Why?

  • As a freelancer I need to spend time with clients, not Google
  • To kickstart operations at scale I needed thousands of emails sent

How?

  • Built a system around nanoclaw linked to my Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions managed via a local dashboard
  • Three groups of agents: 1. researches leads online, 2. scores their SEO and tech stack, 3. drafts targeted outreach and emails it after human review and confirmation
  • Daily status reports via Telegram dms
  • Self-hosted on a network-isolated Raspberry Pi, managed via local SSH with Codex / Claude Code

So?

  • More than 3,000 leads qualified and contacted
  • Immeasurable hours of googling drudgery saved and turned into actual consulting work
